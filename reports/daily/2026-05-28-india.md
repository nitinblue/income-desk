- **Error:** `HTTP Error 403: Host not in allowlist` on every yfinance call
- **Scope:** All 21 India tickers + all US benchmark tickers (SPY, TLT, EEM, ^VIX, ^NSEI, etc.)
- **Impact:** Entire data pipeline dead — scan, regime, rank, and technicals all fail downstream
